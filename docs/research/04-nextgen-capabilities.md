# 2026+ Texture-Painting DCC: Capability Bar & Rust+wgpu Feasibility

**Research basis:** ~25 web searches + primary-source extracts (OpenPBR spec site, MaterialX town-hall 2026 deck, wgpu tracking issues/changelog, Adobe release notes, NeurIPS/CVPR 2025 papers). Key context as of 2026-10: Substance Painter is at 12.1 (OpenPBR default, skew-map painting, auto-rebake); OpenPBR spec v1.1.1 with 1.2 feature-complete; Mari 7.5 (Dec 2025) added Multi-Paint; wgpu at v30 (July 2026) with experimental mesh shaders, ray queries, and HDR surface color spaces.

---

## P0 — Professional floor (cannot ship without)

**1. OpenPBR Surface as the native shader + channel model.**
OpenPBR v1.1.1 supersedes both Standard Surface and ASM; Painter 12.1, Designer 16, Maya 2025.3, and 3ds Max 2026 all default to it, and Adobe is converting all 14,000+ Substance Assets to OpenPBR. Implement the spec's slab/layer formalism (metal, glossy-diffuse, SSS, coat, fuzz, emission, thin-film) as the viewport über-shader and use OpenPBR parameter naming as the texture-channel contract for export. Feasibility: high — the spec has a MaterialX reference implementation and Adobe open-sourced a portable Apache-2.0 C++ BSDF (`openpbr.h`, header-only) that can be transliterated to WGSL; MaterialX itself gained WGSL shader generation in v1.39.4 (2025-09), so codegen is proven. A WGSL port is weeks of shader work, not research. URLs: https://academysoftwarefoundation.github.io/OpenPBR/ , https://github.com/adobe/openpbr-bsdf , https://www.cgchannel.com/2026/06/adobe-releases-substance-3d-painter-12-1/ , https://github.com/AcademySoftwareFoundation/MaterialX/releases/tag/v1.39.4

**2. UDIM / multi-UV-tile support (paint across tiles).**
UDIM (`1001 + u + 10*v`) is the film-industry floor: Mari handles tens of thousands of textures this way and Painter has supported painting across UV tiles since 2019. Requires: per-tile presence/assignment in the texture-set model, stroke continuity across tile boundaries (the stroke must be evaluated in 3D, not per-tile), tile pickers in 2D/3D UI, per-tile resolution (Mari patches allow mixed resolutions), and `<udim>` token in export naming. Feasibility: high — this is data-structure work (tile-indexed `HashMap<(u8,u8), TextureHandle>`), and GPU side is just an offset in the sampling path; Blender's UDIM paint bugs (#155930, seam issues) document the exact pitfalls to avoid (per-tile mask lookup per triangle, not per pixel). URLs: https://www.adobe.com/products/substance3d/magazine/paint-across-uv-tiles-udims-in-substance-painter.html , https://projects.blender.org/blender/blender/issues/155930 , https://www.sidefx.com/docs/houdini/solaris/udim.html

**3. GPU tile-pool texture architecture for 4K–16K (virtual texturing).**
Painter uses Sparse Virtual Textures in its viewport since 2018.3; Mari's "Virtual Texture Atlas" streams only visible regions of huge textures into VRAM. wgpu does **not** expose sparse residency (WebGPU punted it — gpuweb#455; wgpu-hal has no Vulkan sparse binding path), so a manual software VT is mandatory: physical page atlas (e.g. 8192² pool of 256² pages), page-table indirection texture, async page upload on background thread, plus per-channel page pools for the paint targets. Feasibility: medium-high — the pure-Rust pattern is proven by `carloskiki/wgpu-virtual-texturing` (page-pool + indirection, TODO: page-table texture, ASTC); the hard 20% is eviction policy under paint traffic and mip-chain updates. Budget this as a core-engine deliverable, not an add-on. URLs: https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/substance-3d-painter/using/features/sparse-virtual-textures , https://learn.foundry.com/mari/7.5/Content/getting_started_guide/installation_licensing/hardware_use_in_mari.html , https://github.com/carloskiki/wgpu-virtual-texturing , https://github.com/gpuweb/gpuweb/issues/455

**4. High→low poly baking suite (AO, normal, curvature, position, thickness, ID) with skew control.**
Baking is Painter's daily bread; 12.1 (2026) made it continuous (auto-rebake of affected regions), added painted skew correction, and edge protection. Hardware ray tracing is *not* required: a compute-shader BVH or brute-force raycast over a preprocessed BLAS handles high-poly sources at bake time (one-off, seconds not frames). Feasibility: medium — straightforward compute passes in WGSL; the labor is cage/skew/offset controls and matching Painter's quality on thin geometry, which is where its years of tuning live. wgpu's experimental ray queries (Vulkan/DX12/Metal landed) can accelerate this once stable. URLs: https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/substance-3d-painter/using/release-notes/version-12-1 , https://www.cgchannel.com/2026/06/adobe-releases-substance-3d-painter-12-1/ , https://github.com/gfx-rs/wgpu/issues/6762

**5. Layer stack: layers, masks, fill layers, smart materials, per-channel resolution, non-destructive until flatten.**
The layer stack (with mask stacks, anchors, smart materials, per-layer procedural fill via .sbsar-like inputs) is the actual product. Painter 12.0 added in-stack flattening/export of groups; 8K export while 4K in-app. Design stacks as GPU-resident command lists composited in a compute/fragment pass per dirty region. Feasibility: high — this is Rust data-model + WGSL compositing; the risk is scope, since masks/generators need mesh-map inputs (baked AO/curvature) and a noise/pattern library (see P1 node engine). URLs: https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/substance-3d-painter/using/release-notes/version-12-0 , https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/substance-3d-painter/using/getting-started/project-creation

**6. OCIO v2 with ACES 2.0 configs, scene-linear working space.**
VFX Reference Platform CY2026 pins OCIO 2.5.x, which ships built-in ACES 2.0 CG/Studio configs (Blender 4.4+/5.x, Flame 2026, Fusion 20 already load them; OCIO 2.6 landed Sept 2026). A DCC must: tag working space as scene-linear, apply display/view transforms via GPU LUTs (3D + 1D), and honor arbitrary user configs. Feasibility: high — Rust bindings exist and track 2.5.2 (`ocio-rs` 0.2.1, plus `ocio_rs` FFI wrapper); bake the OCIO processor to a shader-side 3D texture once per config change. URL: https://opencolorio.readthedocs.io/en/v2.5.1/releases/ocio%5F2%5F5.html , https://crates.io/crates/ocio-rs , https://github.com/AcademySoftwareFoundation/OpenColorIO-Config-ACES/releases/tag/v4.0.0

**7. Export pipeline: templates/presets, channel packing, UDIM naming, 8K, PNG/EXR/TIFF + format matrix.**
Non-negotiable floor: user-definable output templates (regex-ish `$mesh_$color_<udim>`, packed channels like ORM), per-texture-set export with bit-depth options, and EXR for HDR/float channels. Feasibility: high — pure Rust (image codecs: `image`, `exr`/`openexr` crates); the engineering is template semantics parity, not graphics. URLs: https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/substance-3d-painter/using/export/output-templates/creating-export-presets , https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/substance-3d-painter/using/export/export-window/export-settings

**8. Mesh import: FBX, OBJ, glTF/GLB, USD, Alembic, PLY, DAE.**
Painter's floor. FBX is the pain point (proprietary; use the `fbx`-loader Rust ecosystem or vendor Autodesk FBX SDK via FFI; glTF via `gltf` crate is trivial; USD via bindings below). Feasibility: medium — OBJ/glTF/PLY easy in pure Rust; FBX and Alembic need FFI or tolerant reverse-engineered loaders; USD can go through `rust-usd`/`usd` crates or a usda (text) subset first. URLs: https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/substance-3d-painter/using/features/universal-scene-description-usd , https://crates.io/crates/rust-usd , https://lib.rs/crates/usd

**9. Brush/paint engine: projection + 3D paint, symmetry, seam-aware stamping, stroke streaming.**
Real-time 3D painting needs: 3D-space stroke evaluation (splat stamps onto the tile-pool), lazy-mouse/spacing/stabilization, symmetry across axes, and **seam handling** — stroke fragments crossing UV seams must land consistently in both islands' texel space (Blender's PBVH painting design doc for 16K textures and its seam-bleeding diffs (D14970/D16963) are the best public spec of this; classic solution is texel overscanning/dilation). Feasibility: medium-high — WGSL compute splatting into the page pool; the seam-consistency and per-tile stroke routing logic is the deep water. Mari's paint-buffer (2D buffer between artist and model, baked to surface) is the alternative projection paradigm worth offering too. URLs: https://projects.blender.org/blender/blender/issues/96223 , https://archive.blender.org/developer/differential/0004/0014970/D14970.id58095.html , https://learn.foundry.com/mari/current/Content/user_guide/painting/paint_buffer.html

**10. MaterialX (.mtlx) document export for materials.**
Beyond raster export, a 2026 tool must emit MaterialX documents (the interchange standard consumed by USD/Hydra via UsdMtlx, Houdini/Karma, Octane, and increasingly UE). MaterialX v1.39.5 (May 2026) is integrated into OpenUSD 26.08 and now ships OpenPBR 1.1.1 + Slang gen; the `materialx` Rust crate is a placeholder, so plan on XML parsing of the spec-defined schema (well-documented) rather than the C++ SDK. Feasibility: medium — .mtlx is plain XML with a fully-specified node graph schema; a Rust reader/writer for the subset needed (OpenPBR nodedef + image/tiledimage/UDIM tokens) is tractable without FFI. URLs: https://github.com/AcademySoftwareFoundation/MaterialX/blob/main/documents/Specification/MaterialX.Specification.md , https://materialx.org/assets/ASWF_VTH2026_MaterialX_OpenPBR_Final.pdf , https://materialx.org/ThirdPartySupport.html

## P1 — Differentiators (win users)

**11. USD-native pipeline (UsdMtlx/UsdShade round-trip).**
"OpenPBR everywhere" is Adobe's 2026 message; OpenUSD 26.08 integrates MaterialX natively, and AOUSD (Pixar/Adobe/Apple/Autodesk/NVIDIA alliance) makes USD the pipeline spine. Round-tripping materials (UsdShade → OpenPBR ↔ .mtlx) plus mesh loading via USD is the film-pipeline door-opener. Feasibility: medium — Rust USD bindings (`rust-usd`, `usd`, `pxr_rs`) are functional but immature (docs.rs build failures on some); pragmatic path is usda text import/export first, binary usdc via the C++ SDK FFI later. URLs: https://openusd.org/25.05/api/_page__material_x__in__hydra_u_s_d.html , https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2023/08/pixar-adobe-apple-autodesk-and-nvidia-form-alliance-for-openusd/ , https://crates.io/crates/rust-usd

**12. Ray-traced baking + viewport preview with OIDN2 denoising.**
wgpu ray tracing status: ray **queries** are experimental-but-landed on Vulkan/DX12/Metal; full RT pipelines are an open WIP PR (#9450); tracking issue #6762 shows solid momentum (BLAS compaction, custom AABBs, binding arrays done). For denoising, OIDN 2.x supports GPU (CUDA/HIP/SYCL) and has mature Rust bindings: `oidn` 2.5.1 (TwinkleBear) and even `oidn-wgpu` for direct wgpu interop, including the RT-lightmap filter. Feasibility: medium — ship compute-fallback first (Blender Cycles-style, no RT hardware dependency), gate wgpu ray-query path behind feature detection; OIDN via the `oidn` crate is near turnkey. URLs: https://github.com/gfx-rs/wgpu/issues/6762 , https://github.com/gfx-rs/wgpu/pull/9450 , https://crates.io/crates/oidn , https://github.com/mirkoattiladaniel/oidn-wgpu , https://www.openimagedenoise.org/documentation.html

**13. AI-assisted texturing, local-first.**
Reality check 2026: Adobe's Firefly text-to-texture lives in **Sampler**, not Painter (Painter still has no first-party AI brush; the Steam "does it have AI features?" thread and third-party prompt plugins like TexGenesis calling external APIs confirm the gap — which is *our* opening). The local-first stack: SD-family img2img/inpaint as a "generative brush" (region-masked, running via `candle` — pure-Rust SD inference with CUDA/wgpu backends — or a sidecar ComfyUI); **Tiled Diffusion (CVPR 2025)** for genuinely seamless tileables via user-drawn edge constraints; ControlNet depth/normal conditioning from the model's own mesh maps for paint-what-you-see. The hard part remains **UV-seam awareness**: the state of the art is multi-view synchronized diffusion — TEXTure (SIGGRAPH 2023) → Text2Tex (ICCV 2023) → Paint3D (CVPR 2024, lighting-less 2K maps) → MVPaint (CVPR 2025) → GOATex (NeurIPS 2025, occlusion-aware) → SeqTex (SIGGRAPH Asia 2025, video-sequence texture models). Feasibility: medium for the brush-level (inpaint on the 2D tile through the existing paint pipeline — seam artifacts are the known failure; mitigate with seam-margin blending and dilate); low-medium for full 3D-aware generation on consumer GPUs (multi-GB models, minutes not seconds). Architecture decision: model-agnostic provider interface (local candle / ONNX / remote API) so legal and compute both stay flexible. URLs: https://github.com/madaror/tiled-diffusion , https://mvpaint.github.io/ , https://goatex3d.github.io/ , https://github.com/huggingface/candle/blob/main/candle-examples/examples/stable-diffusion/README.md , https://steamcommunity.com/app/4329260/discussions/0/840628131190584310/

**14. Open procedural node-graph engine (the .sbsar replacement).**
We cannot execute Adobe's format — so make the graph engine *itself* the feature. Prior art: Material Maker (Godot/GLSL, 200+ nodes, JSON `.ptex` files), TexGraph (MIT, C++), Knoodle, TextureLab, NodeMangler, Aqueduct, QuiltiX (Python/Qt MaterialX editor on Hydra viewport), and InstaMAT's free tier. The winning design: author-time graphs serialize directly as **MaterialX documents** (nodes = MaterialX standard nodes; free interchange with Houdini/UE/UsdMtlx, and the WGSL generator from MaterialX 1.39.4 means graphs compile to wgpu shaders), plus example-based synthesis (`EmbarkStudios/texture-synthesis`, pure Rust, multiresolution stochastic synthesis) as a first-class generator primitive — unique, local, no-legal-encumbrance. Feasibility: medium-high — node graph infra (topological sort, dirty-region eval, WGSL codegen or interpretive compute passes) is well-trodden in Rust; parity with 200 Designer nodes is a long tail, so ship 40 core nodes + community extension API. URLs: https://www.materialmaker.org/ , https://github.com/SprueKit/TexGraph , https://github.com/PrismPipeline/QuiltiX , https://github.com/EmbarkStudios/texture-synthesis , https://github.com/AcademySoftwareFoundation/MaterialX/pull/2996

**15. Git-friendly, mergeable project format (the .spp kill-shot).**
Painter's `.spp` is a monolithic HDF5 archive (confirmed by Adobe's own `[Hdf5Archive]` corruption error) — binary, non-diffable, non-mergeable, single-writer. Open-format design: project = directory; `project.json` (layer stack, graph, settings — one JSON object per line or per-layer files for clean diffs), textures as content-hash-named PNG/EXR tiles (`.gitattributes` + LFS or Cas-like store), assets referenced by hash not embedding. Precedent: Unity YAML merge pain, SceneGit (SIGGRAPH Asia 2019, diff/merge for 3D scenes), Forge (format-aware git wrapper with plugin diffs). Feasibility: high — this is serialization discipline from day one, zero graphics risk, and a genuine "why we switched" story; per-layer files make `git merge-file` workable since two artists editing different layers never conflict. URLs: https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/substance-3d-painter/using/technical-support/workflow-issues/project-issues/a-project-has-been-processed-as-a-text-file-and-is-now-corrupted , https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3355089.3356550 , https://github.com/ForgeHubProject/forge

**16. HDR viewport output (HDR10 / scRGB).**
wgpu v30 (July 2026) shipped `SurfaceColorSpace` selection with HDR10 > extended-linear-scRGB > sRGB fallback, implemented across Vulkan/DX12/Metal/WebGPU. With OCIO display transforms this yields an ACES-managed HDR viewport — today only high-end render DCCs bother; no texture painter does. Feasibility: high post-v30 — configure swapchain color space, tone-map in WGSL, present in Rgba16Float; the wgpu HDR example is nearly copy-paste. URLs: https://github.com/gfx-rs/wgpu/pull/9658 , https://wgpu.rs/doc/wgpu/documentation/color/hdr_surfaces/index.html , https://github.com/gfx-rs/wgpu/blob/trunk/examples/standalone/03_hdr_surface/src/main.rs

**17. GPU-driven viewport: mesh shaders/meshlets.**
wgpu v28 shipped experimental mesh shaders (`EXPERIMENTAL_MESH_SHADER`; meshlet culling examples exist). Relevant when viewports exceed ~10M triangles (film-res sculpts, imported CAD); Painter-style assets rarely need it, so treat as viewport-scaling insurance with a compute-culling fallback. Feasibility: medium — the API is experimental and Metal/coverage is still stabilizing (tracking issue #7197); keep the classic vertex path primary, feature-gate meshlets. URLs: https://github.com/gfx-rs/wgpu/releases/tag/v28.0.0 , https://github.com/gfx-rs/wgpu/issues/7197 , https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/introduction-turing-mesh-shaders/

**18. Triplanar painting + auto-unwrap beyond-UV workflows.**
Two faces of "no good UVs": (a) triplanar sampling is a standard shader feature — but *tri-planar painting* (projecting stroke into 3 planar spaces then baking to UVs, with blending weights) is a workflow differentiator; (b) auto-unwrap: Painter 12.1 added a hard-surface auto-UV mode; research is active (PartUV 2025/2026 part-based unwrapping; ARTUV ICLR 2026). Mari's roller brush (paint tileable images along strokes) is another borrowable gem. Feasibility: medium — triplanar eval is WGSL math; auto-unwrap is a geometry-processing problem in Rust (xatlas-style), with the hard-surface ortho-aligned mode as v1. URLs: https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/substance-3d-painter/using/release-notes/version-12-1 , https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2511.16659 , https://learn.foundry.com/mari/Content/user_guide/painting/roller-brush.html

**19. Continuous auto-rebake + skew-map painting.**
Painter 12.1's flagship: mesh-map bakes update automatically when parameters change (region-limited), and artists paint skew corrections directly on the model. This is a workflow-quality bar, not a research bar. Feasibility: high — incremental compute re-execution over dirty tiles using the existing tile-pool; unified undo history between bake and paint modes is the discipline cost. URL: https://www.cgchannel.com/2026/06/adobe-releases-substance-3d-painter-12-1/

## P2 — Moonshot (2+ years out)

**20. Native 3D texture generation in-viewport (MVPaint/SeqTex-class).**
Synchronized multi-view diffusion producing seam-consistent, lighting-free textures is advancing fast (MVPaint CVPR 2025; SeqTex trains native mesh-texture video models; GOATex handles occluded interiors) but still research-grade: heavy models, minutes-per-asset, evaluation still visual. Ship the provider interface in P1, keep the 3D-native generator as a research track. Feasibility: low-medium in-tool — requires stable research + local GPU VRAM (≥16GB comfortable); track papers, don't build on them yet. URLs: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.02336 , https://arxiv.org/html/2507.04285v1 , https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/cd88d62a2063fdaf7ce6f9068fb15dcd-Abstract-Conference.html

**21. Ptex-class per-face texturing / 3D-paint-on-volume.**
Ptex is effectively niche-alive (RenderMan/Houdini/3DCoat support it; Blender's PTEX branch died — "too hardware hungry, not human-readable"; UDIM won the interchange war). Revisit as *internal* representation (seamless per-face pages feeding the tile-pool — it actually composes beautifully with virtual texturing) rather than interchange format. Volumetric paint (3DCoat-style voxel painting, SplatPainting SIGGRAPH 2025 Gaussian-splat brushes) is a different product; don't chase. Feasibility: low priority — the internal per-face representation is plausible (pages are pages), but ecosystem demand is thin. URLs: https://www.sidefx.com/docs/houdini/shade/ptex.html , https://blenderartists.org/t/what-happened-to-blenders-ptex-branch/1199338 , https://github.com/nv-tlabs/SplatPainting

**22. Virtualized-geometry (Nanite-class) paintable viewport.**
Meshlet pipeline + hardware ray queries + VT together would let artists paint directly on film-res sculpts with no decimation — the capstone capability. All three wgpu primitives exist experimentally but composing them (visibility-buffer picking at 100M tris + stroke→texel routing at that density) is multi-year. Feasibility: research — track wgpu mesh-shader and RT-pipeline stabilization (issues #7197, #6760) as gating signals. URLs: https://enginearchitecture.org/downloads/REAC_2025_Capcom.pdf , https://github.com/gfx-rs/wgpu/issues/6760

**23. Multi-user live collaboration (CRDT layer stacks).**
Nobody in this market has it (Painter's cloud story is asset sync, not co-editing). A git-friendly format (P1 #15) plus CRDT semantics on the layer stack (per-layer, per-stroke operations as events) yields true concurrent painting. Feasibility: research-adjacent engineering — CRDTs on ordered trees are a known hard problem (study Yjs/Automerge); only worth it after the single-user product is solid. URLs: https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/substance-3d/general-knowledge/ecosystem/import-and-export-formats , https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3355089.3356550

**24. OpenPBR 1.2+/1.3 features: retro-reflectivity, specular haze, glint NDFs.**
OpenPBR 1.2 is feature-complete (retro-reflective microfacet model — an Autodesk/NVIDIA/Intel collaboration already in MaterialX 1.38.6; specular haze; unbounded specular); the 1.3 roadmap names glint NDFs and foliage BSDFs. These are differentiators that keep the shader "current" but require constant-time glint evaluation research to be viewport-practical. Feasibility: high for 1.2 (spec is public, MaterialX has implementations); research-grade for glints. URL: https://materialx.org/assets/ASWF_VTH2026_MaterialX_OpenPBR_Final.pdf , https://academysoftwarefoundation.github.io/OpenPBR/

## Strategic synthesis

- **The bar has moved:** OpenPBR-default, auto-rebake, skew painting, UDIM everywhere, OCIO/ACES 2.0, USD+MaterialX round-trip = the 2026 floor. AI text-to-texture is *not* in Painter itself — an open local-first AI brush is an open flank.
- **wgpu reality check:** sparse residency: absent (build software VT, P0 #3); ray queries: experimental-landed, RT pipelines WIP (#9450); mesh shaders: experimental since v28; HDR color spaces: shipped v30; bindless: exists but currently *slower* than classic bindings (#6501) — don't architect around it.
- **Legal moat:** .sbsar is untouchable; the play is MaterialX-as-procedural-format + Embark-style synthesis + graph extension API. Every asset we author in that form is *more* portable than Adobe's.
